# Presence

GARDEN_PRESENCE_BODY

The garden uses the same tokens as [www.axelquack.de](https://www.axelquack.de): paper `#f7f7f7`, ink `#0a0a0a`, muted `#8a8a8a`, hairline `--line`. Instrument Serif, Inter, IBM Plex Mono. No brand-colour accent. No cards, pills, or shadows.

## Type

> [!summary]
> Three voices, never four. Display serif for thought. UI sans for chrome. Mono for metadata.

> [!note]
> Emphasis is size, weight, and hairlines — not a hue.

> [!warning]
> Do not add a fourth family, or Inter for display.

> [!todo]
> Keep `src/tokens.css`, `docs/tokens.json`, and `docs/design.md` in lockstep.

The reading column stacks at 720px. Motion is `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
